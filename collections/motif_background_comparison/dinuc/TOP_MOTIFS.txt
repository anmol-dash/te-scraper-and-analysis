TOP MOTIFS BY FAMILY
====================
JASPAR CORE vertebrates, MOODS p < 0.0001, both strands.
Enrichment is against a dinucleotide-preserving shuffle of the same
copies, so GC/CpG content is controlled for and what remains is order.

'binds' is the JASPAR matrix consensus, trimmed to its informative
core; 'observed' is built from the bases these copies actually carry.
IUPAC: R=A/G Y=C/T S=C/G W=A/T K=G/T M=A/C B=not A D=not C H=not G V=not T N=any

Read with three caveats:
  * JASPAR is redundant -- adjacent rows are often one TF family, not
    independent hits.
  * A PWM match is a sequence match, NOT evidence of binding, and is
    not tissue-specific on its own.
  * Fragmented families depress %copies; read hits/kb alongside it.
